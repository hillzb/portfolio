Water Case Study

I developed a website for the User Experience design class for the Indigenous Friends Association IndigiTECH Program. The assignment was to look at a problem in your community that could use a solution.

I picked the Boil Water Advisory Crisis in Canada. I wanted to highlight the inadequacies in the Federal Government failing to provide First Nation's communities with clean drinking water.
