Water Case Study

Through the User Experience Design Exploration class at IFA, I was able to learn about and apply UX/UI principles to a problem in the local community.

The problem was the frequent Boil Water Advisories in Oneida Nation of The Thames. For a long time, the people of Oneida were told that their water was not clean for drinking. They would have to rely on other water sources, as their tap water was not fit for drinking.

I picked the Boil Water Advisory Crisis in Canada. I wanted to highlight the inadequacies in the Federal Government failing to provide First Nation's communities with clean drinking water.
